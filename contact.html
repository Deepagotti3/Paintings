Common Questions

Before You Reach Out

Are all paintings truly original?

Every single work is a one-of-a-kind original. No prints, no reproductions, no editions. Each painting comes with a signed certificate of authenticity documenting the work's provenance.

How are paintings packaged for shipping?

Works on canvas are either rolled (for large works) or corner-protected and wrapped in acid-free tissue, then double-boxed. Framed and glazed works are additionally crated in custom wooden cases.

What is your returns policy?

If a painting doesn't suit your space, you may return it within 30 days of delivery in its original packaging for a full refund. Commissioned works are non-refundable once work has begun.

How long does a commission take?

Depending on size and complexity, commissions typically take 4 to 12 weeks from concept approval. I take a limited number of commissions per year to ensure full attention to each work.

Do you offer payment instalments?

Yes. For works above ₹30,000, a 50% deposit secures the painting and the balance is payable before dispatch. For commissions, 50% is required upfront and 50% on completion.

Can I see a painting in person?

Studio visits are welcome by appointment in Bengaluru. For collectors elsewhere, high-resolution photographs in different lighting conditions and a video walkthrough of the work can be arranged.
